# MyRapid KL Pluse Decision Support System (DSS)

An application designed to help public transportation users to find other alternative routes during unexpected disruption service. it provide a routes suggestion by consider of train delays, estimated trave times(ETA) and station crowd levels

## Problem Statement
when the train and buses are disrupted, transportation users may find it difficult to find other way to reach the destination. Although the service provides information about the delays, the passanger may still need to seach for other routes manually and to know the crowd levels.
